# Day 02 — Structure of a Research Paper

## 1. Title
Tells us what the research is about.

## 2. Abstract
A short summary of the entire research paper, including the problem, methodology, experiments and results.

## 3. Introduction
Explains the background, importance and motivation of the research problem.

## 4. Literature Review
Studies and analyses what other researchers have already done about the research topic.

## 5. Research Gap
Identifies unanswered questions, limitations or unexplored areas in existing research.

## 6. Methodology
Explains how the researchers conducted their research, including methods, algorithms, datasets and procedures.

## 7. Experiments and Results
Describes the experiments, evaluation metrics, collected results and their analysis.

## 8. Conclusion
Summarizes the findings, contributions, limitations and possible future research.

## Research Paper Reading Sequence

Title → Abstract → Introduction → Literature Review → Research Gap → Methodology → Experiments and Results → Conclusion.
