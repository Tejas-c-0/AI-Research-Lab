# AI Research Lab — Learning Path

This guide tells you **which Markdown file to read first, what to understand, and what to do before moving on**. Follow the sequence. Do not jump to advanced algorithms before the data and research foundations are clear.

> The sequence below is based on the actual Markdown notes currently in this repository. New notes will be added to this guide when they are reviewed.

## How to study each topic

For every file:

1. Read the complete note once without trying to memorise it.
2. Re-read each definition and explain it in your own words.
3. Recreate the diagrams or examples without looking.
4. Answer the practice questions independently.
5. Write down anything you could not explain.
6. Move forward only when you can explain the main idea and give a fresh example.

## What comes after these notes?

The next research-focused modules should be developed in this order:

1. **Problem formulation for ML research** — prediction task, research question, scope, and contribution.
2. **Literature search and review** — search strategy, paper screening, evidence tables, and synthesis.
3. **ML foundations needed for research** — regression, classification, clustering, baselines, and when each is appropriate.
4. **Experimental design** — datasets, splits, leakage prevention, controls, and reproducibility.
5. **Evaluation and statistics** — metric selection, uncertainty, confidence intervals, cross-validation, and error analysis.
6. **Research writing** — methods, results, discussion, limitations, citations, and responsible claims.
7. **Reproducible research project** — a complete documented study from question to report.
